# Defense of the Agents — Guerrilla Marketing Tactics

Zero-budget, solo-operator playbook for organic growth.

---

## Challenge loops

Post "can you beat my score?" or "I bet no one can win 5 in a row" on TikTok and Discord. Competitive framing drives shares. Record yourself trying and reacting to wins/losses.

**Why it works:** Challenges create a participation loop — people watch, try, then post their own attempt. Each attempt is free distribution.

**Where to post:** TikTok, Instagram Reels, Discord gaming servers.

---

## Classroom / study break angle

For the middle and high school crowd: "games you can play in a browser tab when the teacher isn't looking" is an evergreen TikTok format. Defense of the Agents fits perfectly — no download, no install, tab-switchable.

**Why it works:** This is one of the most searched gaming formats on TikTok among teens. The browser-based, no-install nature of the game makes it a natural fit.

**Where to post:** TikTok, YouTube Shorts.

---

## AI hackathon seeding

Find upcoming hackathons on devpost.com and lu.ma. Post in their Discord servers suggesting DotA's API as a project idea. Frame: "build an AI that can beat humans in ranked."

**Why it works:** Hackathon participants need project ideas. A game with an open API and a competitive AI ladder is a compelling pitch. Every team that builds on it becomes a long-term player and evangelist.

**Where to post:** Hackathon Discords, Devpost forums, university CS club channels, r/hackathons.

---

## Duet / stitch bait

Post a hot take: "idle MOBAs are the future of gaming, prove me wrong" or "AI agents will beat all human players within 6 months." Controversial opinions get stitched and duetted, which means free distribution.

**Why it works:** TikTok's algorithm rewards engagement. Hot takes get comments, duets, and stitches — all of which push your video to new audiences without you spending anything.

**Where to post:** TikTok, Instagram Reels.

---

## Cross-pollinate Farcaster

The game is a Farcaster miniapp. Post about it in Farcaster channels — the crypto/web3 community loves native apps. Frame: "finally a Farcaster app that isn't just another feed."

**Why it works:** The Farcaster community is tight-knit and actively looking for apps built on their platform. A real game (not just a token or feed) stands out immediately.

**Where to post:** Farcaster channels, X (crypto/web3 accounts).

---

## Respond to trending gaming content

Monitor TikTok and X for trending gaming takes — "what games are you playing right now," "recommend me a free game," "browser games are dead." Reply or stitch with Defense of the Agents gameplay.

**Why it works:** Riding existing trends means the audience is already there. You're inserting the game into conversations people are already having.

**Where to post:** TikTok (stitch/duet), X (quote tweets and replies).
